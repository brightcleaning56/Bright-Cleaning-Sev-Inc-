# Goldfinch Lofts (3404 Ingersoll Ave) - cleaning scope from the drawings

Source: "Goldfinch Lofts - Revision 4 - Combined" construction documents, dated 08/04/2025 (156 pages, 128 MB PDF).
Read from the PDF's text layer. Floor plans and elevations are vector drawings, so unit counts and window areas were not read.

## Building
- Three-story mixed-use: core and shell retail on Level 1 (A-2 assembly, M mercantile); apartments on Levels 2 and 3 (R-2). Fully sprinklered.
- Exterior materials seen in the notes and specs: aluminum storefront with brake-metal cladding (Level 1), polymer window systems (Levels 2 and 3), wood siding (1x6 T&G vertical, matching CLT panels), CLT panels, brick and masonry, perforated-metal balcony guardrails and soffits (painted, 16 ga, 35% open area), and membrane roofing with roof decks.
- Balconies at Levels 2 and 3, north and south (sheets A6.04, A6.05).
- Trash enclosure (sheet A6.12) and a courtyard with paving and retaining walls (landscape sheets L3.00 to L3.02).

## Windows (sheet A7.03 window schedule)
- 113 window marks in total:
  - Level 1: W100 to W128, 29 aluminum storefront units (types G, H, I, J, K, L, M, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T). Glass marked "T" is tempered.
  - Level 2: W200 to W241, 42 polymer units (types A, B, C, D, F), most with insect screens.
  - Level 3: W300 to W341, 42 polymer units (same types).
- Operable windows with a sill under 36" have restrictors (no 4" sphere passage).
- Interior and exterior glass is cleaned together. Exterior work is at heights up to three stories.
- Polymer frames and storefront aluminum are cleaned with a soft wash; the glass is the main job.

## Window-washing access
- The structural notes (sheet S0.00) require a contractor-designed tieback, davit, or building maintenance unit (BMU) system, with a sealed engineer letter.
- The drawings show no permanent window-washing anchors, davit, or BMU, so exterior glass will need a ladder or lift (or a contractor-supplied system).
- Confirm this with the architect before pricing the exterior work.

## Building exterior cleaning
- Painted perforated-metal balconies and soffits: low-pressure wash, no high pressure, so the coating is not damaged.
- Wood siding and CLT: soft wash; check whether the wood has a finish or clear coat.
- Brick and masonry: soft wash, and check for efflorescence.
- Storefront and cladding: soft wash, with the frames and sills cleaned separately.
- Roof and roof drains: clean drains and gutters where they are accessible. Membrane roofing: check the roofing spec before using any cleaner.
- Trash enclosure: pressure wash, with runoff controlled (see the site plan and storm details).
- Concrete: the structural notes (CM-4) require a regular maintenance program for cracks and water entry; a maintenance schedule should be provided to the owner.
- Sealants: 36 sealant references. Do not wash with a high-pressure wash near sealant joints.

## Interior notes relevant to cleaning
- Floor finishes include carpet (CPT-1), ceramic or tile (CT-1), and rubber tile in the common areas.
- Balconies are open (no glass balcony rails), so cleaning is open-air and needs a fall-protection plan on the upper levels.
