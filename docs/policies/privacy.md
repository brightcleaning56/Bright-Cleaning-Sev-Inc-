BUILDINTEL PRIVACY NOTICE

What we collect
1. Subscriber account data: name, business email, company, role, login records.
2. Public construction records: permit numbers, addresses, project descriptions, valuations, square footage and contractor names published by government agencies. Each record links to its source.
3. Business contact information: names, job titles, business emails and business phone numbers of people at general contractors and property firms, collected only from official or company-published sources. Each contact record stores the evidence link. We do not collect home addresses, personal financial data, government ID numbers or sensitive personal characteristics.
4. Communications records: proposals sent through the platform, delivery events, unsubscribe and suppression records.

Why we use it
To identify commercial projects, prepare estimates and proposals, deliver bids, keep suppression lists, bill subscribers, secure the service and meet legal obligations.

What we do not do
We do not sell subscriber pricing, proposals or customer lists. One subscriber never sees another subscriber's pricing, proposals, contacts or messages.

Your choices
Anyone can request access, correction, deletion or opt-out at /api/privacy/requests or by emailing [SECURITY_CONTACT]. Opt-outs take effect immediately platform-wide. We respond to other requests within 45 days.

Retention
Contact records: until outdated, deleted on request, or 24 months without verification. Sent proposals and delivery evidence: 7 years (bid records). Suppression records: kept indefinitely so opt-outs are honored. Backups: 30 days rolling, encrypted.

Security
Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest for backups, hashed passwords, role-based access, tenant isolation, audit logs.
