## 2. Questions for counsel

### Securities
1. Which exemption, if any, should the first raise use (for example, private placement limited to accredited investors)? The project plans a $150K–$300K first round, then $0.75M–$1.8M and $3.5M–$9.5M.
2. Confirm the Regulation Crowdfunding limits before any public offering: $5,000,000 in a rolling 12-month period, and the per-investor limit for non-accredited investors (owner-confirmed as $124,000 per 12 months; original source was a secondary summary).
3. Is the landing page permitted to describe the funding stages and invite contact? What disclaimers and gating are required?
4. What may the deck state about returns? The base case shows about 1.3x at a 35% stake and a 3x exit, with no cash returned before exit. Confirm the wording and any required risk disclosure.
5. Is a SAFE, priced equity, or a convertible instrument the right vehicle for a concept-stage company?
6. Confirm the stake and valuation assumptions. A 35% stake for $10.6M implies about $19.6M pre-money, which is a placeholder and needs a valuation advisor's view.
7. What records must be kept of who received which document, and under what agreement?

### Entity and IP
8. The legal name is DentistNow. Should the robotics work be a division, a trade name ("DentistNow Robotics"), or a separate subsidiary of DentistNow? Proposed: a Delaware corporation, matching DentistNow's existing Delaware registration. The Iowa program ($75K total support) requires an Iowa-based company, so it is only available if the company will operate in Iowa. Confirm which applies.
9. What invention-assignment and NDA templates are needed for employees, contractors, research partners and advisors?
10. Scope of a patentability and freedom-to-operate search before showing designs to manufacturers.

### Claims and regulatory language
11. Review all statements about FDA status, clinical performance, and what the machine does. The documents say the product is not cleared and is not for clinical use. Confirm this language is sufficient.
12. Review the "unattended" and "autonomous" language and the scope limits (no drilling, extraction, restorative or subgingival treatment in version 1).
13. Review any patient data handling, including the UI prototype's sample records and the privacy notice required before the landing page goes live.

### Grants
14. Confirm the Iowa program terms and eligibility, and any conflict between the Iowa program and an SBIR/STTR application.
15. Confirm there is no restriction on applying for NIDCR SBIR funding while the company is an investor-funded entity.
