# Data room index

Purpose: which materials can be shared, with whom, and at what gate. Nothing leaves the team until securities counsel has approved the recipient group and the materials for it.

## Gate levels

| Level | Who may see it | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| G0 — Internal | Founders and core team | None |
| G1 — Advisors | Clinical and regulatory advisors under NDA | NDA signed |
| G2 — Grant reviewers | Program officers and grant partners | Program submission; no investor claims |
| G3 — Prospective investors | Accredited or otherwise permitted investors | Counsel approval of offering documents |
| G4 — Public | Anyone | Counsel approval, privacy notice, verified figures |

## Not to share

- Anything containing patient data (none exists yet; keep it that way for the prototype stage).
- Unpublished patent or invention disclosures before a patentability search.
- Supplier quotes and cost estimates marked as estimates until replaced with quotations.
- Internal risk notes, unless counsel has reviewed them.

## Before moving any item up a level

1. Confirm every figure it contains against its source, and record the check date.
2. Remove or label any statement that implies FDA clearance, approval or validated clinical performance.
3. Confirm the recipient is in the right group and under the right agreement.
4. Keep a record of what was sent to whom.
