## Scope rules the package follows

- Examination comes before cleaning. Eligibility gates stop the workflow on any unresolved clinical concern.
- AI findings are labelled **Observed**, **Suspected** or **Not assessable**. None are presented as diagnoses.
- The AI never commands actuators directly. A separate safety controller can stop motion and retract instruments
  without software, cloud or AI.
- Automated work is limited to validated cleaning and polishing. No drilling, extraction, restorative work,
  or subgingival calculus removal in version 1.
- Instruments use a clean pathway and a closed contaminated-return pathway, with validated reprocessing.
