DentistNow AutoClean Pro 2.0 — Engineering Planning Binder

40 planning sheets across 10 development stages · Tabloid (11×17 in) printable · Revision 0.1 (concept stage)
Status: concept planning document, not manufacturing drawings. No sheet in this binder is a dimensioned CAD drawing or an approved specification. Dimensions shown elsewhere are illustrative and must be replaced by engineered values. Nothing here is FDA-cleared, and nothing here authorizes clinical use. The machine is designed for examination support and validated, professionally supervised cleaning only; it does not perform diagnosis, extraction, drilling, restorative work or subgingival treatment.

How to use this binder

Each sheet defines what must be drawn or specified, which subsystem it belongs to, and the acceptance criterion that the engineering team must meet before the sheet is marked complete. Sheets are printed in order. Sheet numbers are stable identifiers used in the requirements, test plans and change records.

Three operating modes the machine must support

Mode 1 First-visit examination Mode 2 Authorized cleaning Mode 3 Follow-up & monitoring

Architecture: three independent control layers

LayerResponsibilityMay it move the instruments?
AI intelligenceInterprets scans and images, proposes findings and permitted workflow steps, flags uncertainty.No. It proposes; it never commands actuators directly.
Real-time motion controllerExecutes approved motion inside validated physical limits (position, speed, force, workspace).Yes, only inside limits set by the safety layer.
Independent safety controllerHardware e-stop, watchdog, force/motion/visibility interlocks, safe retraction. Works without cloud or AI.It can stop motion and retract at any time. It is never overridden by software.

Patient-visit workflow (Mode 1 → Mode 2)

1 Arrive & identify→2 Medical screen→3 Symptoms→ 4 Oral photos→5 3D scan→6 Periodontal measures→ 7 Radiographs (only if clinician-directed)→8 AI analysis→ 9 Professional review→10 Eligibility gate→11 Consent→ 12 Authorization gate→13 Approved cleaning→14 Report & follow-up

Any unresolved pain, swelling, bleeding pattern, suspected infection, significant periodontal finding or uncertain image at steps 2–10 stops the workflow and routes the patient to a licensed professional. The machine does not continue past an uncertain step.

Sheets 05–08
SHEET 05
Chair assembly (exploded)

Accept: static load case documented for the rated patient mass.
SHEET 06
Positioning mechanism & actuators
  • Recline, height, tilt and headrest actuator placement
  • Limit switches and encoders

Patient access & emergency release

  • Patient stop control reach and placement
Accept: patient can stop motion within one reach without vision.
