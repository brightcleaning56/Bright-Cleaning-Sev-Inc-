DentistNow Robotics

AutoClean Pro 2.0

An AI-assisted dental examination and robotic preventive-cleaning platform. Seeking Stage A feasibility funding.

Confidential planning draft · v0.1
1
The problem

Routine dental prevention depends on scarce clinical hours

  • Preventive visits consume dentist and hygienist time that could go to more complex care.
  • Access is limited for patients with mobility limits, anxiety, or no nearby clinic.
  • Visit quality varies between clinicians and between sites; records are often incomplete.

Market sizing is not yet validated. Stage A includes 20 buyer interviews to test these points.

2
The solution

Examine first, clean only what is cleared

1 · Intake and screen

Identity, medical history, medications, symptoms per tooth or region.

2 · Assess

Oral photos, 3D scan, AI findings labelled Observed, Suspected or Not assessable.

3 · Gate

Eligibility rules stop the visit and refer to a professional when uncertain.

4 · Authorize and clean

Professional authorization, patient consent, validated polishing and plaque removal, full report.

3
Safety by design

AI proposes. Independent hardware decides.

AI intelligence

Interprets images and proposes findings. Cannot command the actuators.

Motion controller

Moves instruments only within validated position, speed and force limits.

Safety controller

Designed to provide a hardware e-stop, watchdog and safe retraction, working without cloud or AI. Testing is part of Stage A.

Version 1: a clinician authorizes each cleaning, and the patient cannot start one. Out of scope: drilling, extraction, restorative work, and subgingival calculus removal.

13
Investor return

How investors get their money back

  • No cash back before exit in the Base case. Cumulative cash stays negative through Year 7.
  • Exit is the main route. Base-case enterprise value $36.7M in Year 7 (3x revenue). An investor holding 35% receives about $13.9M, or 1.32x.
  • A royalty does not repay the raise. 5% of Base-case recurring revenue in Years 4–7 is about $0.33M.
  • 3x needs about 126 machines in Year 7, about 1.8x the Base case, or a combination of higher volume, lower unit cost and higher recurring revenue.

Valuation placeholder: $10.6M for 35% implies about $19.6M pre-money, which is high for a concept-stage company. Not for discussion until counsel and a valuation advisor review it.
