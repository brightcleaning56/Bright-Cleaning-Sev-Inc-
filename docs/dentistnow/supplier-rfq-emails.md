# Supplier request-for-quote emails (ready to send)

Approved by the owner. The assistant cannot send email; the owner or an engineering lead sends each one. Before sending: fill in the sender name and title, and attach only the items marked for each supplier. Do not attach the full engineering binder or any CAD file until a confidentiality agreement is signed.

Common terms for every request: quote validity 60 days; unit prices at 1, 10 and 50 units; lead time; minimum order; whether the part is medical-grade or needs medical certification; and a short statement of any restriction on use.

---

## 1. Medical chairs and motion modules
**To:** [suppliers of medical dental chairs and motion modules]
**Subject:** Quote request — motorized patient chair motion module (research prototype)

We are developing a dental-model research prototype and are looking for a motorized chair or motion module that can be integrated with a robotic positioning system. We need: motorized recline, height and headrest positioning; position feedback; limit switches; and emergency release. Please quote (a) a donor chair with a motion module that can be integrated with our controls, and (b) the motion module alone. Please tell us which elements carry medical certification and which do not.

Attachment: section 1 of the quote request list.
