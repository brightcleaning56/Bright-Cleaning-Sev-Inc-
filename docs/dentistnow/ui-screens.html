DentistNow AutoClean Pro 2.0 — UI Screens

Static design mockups for the patient interface and the clinical operator interface. Values shown are sample data. Every finding is labelled Observed, Suspected or Not assessable — never presented as a diagnosis. A stop control is visible on every screen during treatment.

Design status: concept mockups for planning and usability review. They are not validated software, have not been through human-factors testing, and must not be used on patients. Clinical wording and gate logic require review by a licensed dental professional and the regulatory specialist.

Accessibility targets for the production UI: minimum 16 px body text on the patient screens, 44 px touch targets, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, and a non-visual stop path (voice or physical button). These targets are to be verified in usability testing, not assumed.
