# DRAFT: refund and cancellation terms (for counsel review)

**Not for customers. Do not publish or send.** This draft gives counsel a starting point. Every bracketed value is a proposal from the business plan or a placeholder for the owner to decide. Counsel reviews the final wording before the first paying customer.

## 1. Subscriptions (monthly service)
- **Start:** Service starts on the agreed start date. Monthly billing runs on the same day each month.
- **Cancelling:** The customer may cancel at any time by written notice. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. [Proposal: 30 days' written notice, effective at the end of that period.]
- **Charges after cancellation:** No charge for months after the cancellation takes effect.
- **Refunds:** [Proposal: no refund for the current month once service has started; full refund of any prepaid month not yet started.]
- **Visits not used:** [Owner to decide: whether unused visits in a month roll over (plan: not specified).]
- **Extra visits above the monthly allowance:** Billed at the per-visit price in the agreement. [Owner to set the price; see the extra-visit decision in the software plan.]

## 2. Setup fee (one time)
- [Proposal: non-refundable once setup has started. Refundable in full if the customer cancels before setup starts.]

## 3. Pilot agreements
- Pilot terms: [Proposal from the plan: three months at 50% off the monthly price, setup fee at full price, in return for feedback and a reference.]
- Ending a pilot early: [Owner to decide: notice period and whether the discount is kept.]
- The pilot is a service agreement, not a trial. Counsel to confirm wording.

## 4. Data on cancellation
- The company can export its records on request and at cancellation. [Plan: export on request and at cancellation.]
- Deletion on request within [period to be set by counsel] after cancellation. [Plan: to be confirmed by counsel.]

## 5. Payments
- Card payments go through Stripe. Stripe fees are not refunded when a payment is refunded. [Counsel to confirm.]
- Sales tax: [Accountant to confirm how it applies to software subscriptions in Iowa.]

## 6. Points for counsel
- Whether automatic monthly billing needs specific consent wording.
- Wording for cancellation notice, including an email address for notices.
- Limitation of liability and the data processing agreement (the plan lists both as open items).
- Whether call recording and AI phone use need specific consent language.
