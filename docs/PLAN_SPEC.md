## 1. What customers pick

**Home plan (houses and apartments, one to five bedrooms)**
- Rooms and tasks, each with a price per visit:

| Task | Price per visit | Labor time |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen (counters, sinks, appliance fronts, floors) | $27 | 0.9 hr |
| Bathroom, each (toilet, shower, sink, mirror, floor) | $18 | 0.6 hr |
| Floors (vacuum and mop) | $24 | 0.8 hr |
| Bedroom, each | $15 | 0.5 hr |
| Living room (dusting, vacuum, floors) | $15 | 0.5 hr |
| Dusting | $12 | 0.4 hr |

- Quantities: bathrooms 0 to 6, bedrooms 0 to 8.
- Frequency: monthly (1 visit), biweekly (2 visits), or weekly (4 visits) per month.
- Deep clean question: no, yes, or not sure. A deep clean is never in the monthly price. If yes or not sure, it's quoted separately after a walkthrough.

**Apartment plan and Office plan**
- Owner approved (2026-11-19): no instant price. The planner page shows a "Request an apartment or office quote" button to /request-a-quote.html. Prices are set after a walkthrough.
- Draft tables stay in BUSINESS_PLAN.md section 5 until the owner approves them for use.

## 2. How prices are set

- Labor rate: $17.50 per hour (middle of the $15 to $20 range). With the 20% burden, $21.00 per hour.
- Price per billable hour: $21.00 ÷ (1 − 0.30) = $30.00 per hour (30% target margin, from the business plan).
- Each task price = labor hours × $30.
- Minimum charge: $60 per visit.
- Monthly price = price per visit × visits per month.
- Full 1-bedroom package (kitchen, bathroom, floors, bedroom, living room, dusting) = $111 per month, which matches the business plan's $110 figure for a 1 bed / 1 bath.

## 3. Things that are NOT in the planner price

Per the Plans page, these are quoted separately:
- Deep cleaning and vacant-unit preparation
- Excessive buildup
- Inside appliances, interior windows, carpet cleaning, wall spot cleaning
- Specialty services (power washing, parking lot, window work)
- Extra cleaning after an event, move, or party
