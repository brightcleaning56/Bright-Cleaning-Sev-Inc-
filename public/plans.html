Price and scope

If the plan costs more or less than you expected, we can adjust it.

We do not quote a price until we know the space. If the price is higher than you expected, or you want a lower cost, these are the changes we can make, one at a time or together:

To lower the cost

  • Fewer visits a month (for example, weekly to every two weeks, or every two weeks to monthly)
  • Leave out areas you do not need cleaned, such as a guest room or storage
  • Use your own cleaning supplies
  • Choose a flexible time window, so we can fit around other work
  • Sign a longer agreement (for example, six or twelve months)

To add more

  • More visits (for example, adding a second weekly visit)
  • Extras such as inside appliances, interior windows, carpet cleaning, or wall spot cleaning
  • A deep clean on a set schedule, for example every three months
  • Extra cleaning after an event, a move, or a party
  • Walkthroughs and reports for your own records

Changes are agreed in writing before they take effect, and the next invoice reflects them.
