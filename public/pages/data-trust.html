OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Built for accountable decisions.

The platform should make the status and limits of each workflow clear—not hide them behind a polished interface.

Source terms matter.

A public endpoint is not permission to republish every record or drawing.

Integrations must be explicit.

Commercial data integrations require authorized licensing and credentials.

Operational status must be honest.

Payment, email, AI and storage providers are not described as live unless the deployment is actually configured.
