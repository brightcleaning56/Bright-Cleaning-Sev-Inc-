The bid cycle

Invite, collect, compare, award

1

Invite

Invitations go to subscribers whose trades and territory match, including companies that join later.

2

Collect

Bidders submit an amount, scope, exclusions and documents, and can revise while bids are open.

3

Compare

See every bid side by side, with the plan addenda each bidder has acknowledged.

4

Award

Award with an e-signature and keep a contract record, with the signer and time, for both sides.
