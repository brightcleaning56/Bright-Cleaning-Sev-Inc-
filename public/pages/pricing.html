OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Built for accountable decisions.

The platform should make the status and limits of each workflow clear—not hide them behind a polished interface.

Pricing is proposed, not a live offer.

Confirm final price points, limits, taxes, renewals and cancellation terms before publishing checkout.

Stripe is not connected in the reviewed build.

Do not assume self-service payment or subscription changes are operational until the live integration is configured and tested.

No paywall overrides document rights.

A paid subscription does not grant access to a publisher’s restricted files.
