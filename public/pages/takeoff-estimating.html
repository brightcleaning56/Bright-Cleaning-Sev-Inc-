OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Built for accountable decisions.

The platform should make the status and limits of each workflow clear—not hide them behind a polished interface.

Unprinted area is never invented.

If a drawing does not state a value and it cannot be measured, the system must not fabricate it.

AI output is not an approved takeoff.

A qualified estimator must confirm extracted quantities and assumptions.

Keep exact-cent math.

Totals, markups and rounding should be deterministic and reproducible.
