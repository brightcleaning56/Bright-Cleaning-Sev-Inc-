OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Built for accountable decisions.

The platform should make the status and limits of each workflow clear—not hide them behind a polished interface.

Project data and bid invitations are different.

A record can be useful for discovery without being an active solicitation.

Access and rights are separate.

Subscriptions do not override publisher permissions or source terms.

Human accountability remains central.

AI-assisted information and bid recommendations require appropriate review.
