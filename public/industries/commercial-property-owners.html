Industry solutions

Commercial Property Owners & Managers

Commercial property owners and managers need consistent presentation, predictable schedules, and a clear way to raise and resolve service issues. The service specification should identify the areas, tasks, frequencies, and inspection standards.

Request a Commercial Proposal →

Services commonly aligned to this sector

  • Office and commercial janitorial
  • Lobby, corridor, restroom, and common-area cleaning
  • Interior glass and entrance cleaning
  • Power washing and exterior appearance tasks
  • Parking lot litter pickup and recurring site rounds

Information needed to scope the work

  • Building address, size, use, and operating hours
  • Room/area list and floor types
  • Restroom and breakroom counts
  • Required service frequency and service windows
  • Consumables, equipment, waste, and access responsibilities
