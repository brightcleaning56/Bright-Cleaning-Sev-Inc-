Industry solutions

Facility & Operations Managers

Facility managers need cleaning services that respect building operations, access controls, occupancy, and internal service-level expectations. Scope design should reflect critical spaces, operating hours, and security constraints.

Request a Commercial Proposal →

Services commonly aligned to this sector

  • Recurring janitorial and restroom cleaning
  • Breakroom, meeting room, and circulation cleaning
  • Floor maintenance and touchpoint cleaning as scoped
  • Interior glass and entry presentation
  • Project and periodic deep-clean tasks

Information needed to scope the work

  • Facility floor plan and room inventory
  • Operating hours, occupancy, and restricted zones
  • Security, badging, escort, and background requirements
  • Service-level requirements and inspection forms
  • Supply, equipment, waste, and sustainability standards
