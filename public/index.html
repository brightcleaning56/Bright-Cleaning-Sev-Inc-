How we support your project

From bid documents to final walkthrough.

The website is designed to make it easier for a commercial buyer to understand the process before requesting a proposal.

01 · Scope review

Review drawings, specifications, square footage, finish schedules, site conditions, bid instructions, and cleaning expectations.

02 · Site coordination

Clarify site access, safety orientation, work windows, construction phasing, utilities, staging, and points of contact.

03 · Proposal & pricing

Document included tasks, exclusions, assumptions, alternates, schedule, pricing, and commercial terms.

04 · Mobilization

Confirm written authorization, required vendor documents, access, schedule, and project communication.

05 · Quality review

Use agreed checklists, record exceptions, coordinate authorized touch-ups, and confirm closeout.

06 · Account support

For recurring contracts, align service schedules, inspection cadence, issue escalation, and periodic scope reviews.

Quality & safety framework

Defined expectations—not vague promises.

Every project should establish what is included, how work areas are released, which safety rules apply, how completion is inspected, and how deficiencies are resolved.

  • Project-specific scope and acceptance criteria
  • Site orientation, PPE, access and hazard controls
  • Area-based quality checks and issue logs
  • Documented exceptions and authorized corrections

Important project controls

Before mobilization, confirm:

  • Signed agreement or purchase order
  • Approved scope and schedule
  • Insurance and vendor onboarding
  • Site access and safety orientation
  • Named client and field contacts
  • Closeout and invoice requirements
