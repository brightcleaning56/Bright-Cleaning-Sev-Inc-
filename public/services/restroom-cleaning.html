Commercial Restroom Cleaning

Scheduled restroom cleaning for commercial and shared facilities, based on occupancy, fixture count, access, and hygiene requirements.

Scope and planning

Cleaning and disinfection of agreed restroom fixtures and touchpoints, mirrors, partitions, sinks, floors, waste receptacles, and replenishment tasks where supplies are included.

Frequency and task detail are set by restroom count, traffic, operating hours, and the client’s required cleaning standard.

Confirm before pricing

  • Area count and measurements
  • Surface type and current condition
  • Occupancy, traffic, and access window
  • Supply and equipment responsibilities
  • Inspection criteria and frequency

Scope boundaries

  • Specialty restoration or hazardous remediation requires separate review
  • Restricted or occupied areas require authorization
  • Unlisted supplies and consumables are not assumed included
  • Additional work requires written approval
