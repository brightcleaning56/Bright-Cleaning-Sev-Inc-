How website inquiry information is handled.

Information submitted

When a visitor submits a bid request, contact inquiry, or career interest form, the website may collect contact details, company information, project details, uploaded documents, and the information entered in the form.

How information may be used

  • To respond to project bids, service inquiries, and employment interest.
  • To review project scope, prepare proposals, and coordinate site visits.
  • To maintain inquiry records and administer customer relationships.
  • To protect the website, investigate misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Uploaded documents

Bid documents may contain confidential project information. Submit only information you are authorized to share. The company should restrict access, protect stored files, establish retention and deletion schedules, and use malware scanning before production.

Service providers and disclosures

Hosting, email, CRM, storage, analytics, and security providers may process information on the company’s behalf when configured. The company must list actual providers and explain any legally required disclosures.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as needed for business, contractual, and legal purposes. Reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards should be used. No website can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and questions

For questions or requests relating to submitted information, contact info@brightclean.net. Add the company’s verified mailing address and any required privacy request process before launch.

Updates

Update this notice when the company’s forms, systems, service providers, or legal requirements change.
