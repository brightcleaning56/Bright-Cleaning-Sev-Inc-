Bid intake checklist

A complete scope helps us prepare a responsible proposal.

Upload your bid documents and describe the work areas, project schedule, site rules, and required closeout standard.

For construction bids, include

  • Bid invitation, plans, specifications, and addenda
  • Building area, floor count, room/finish schedules
  • Rough clean, final clean, and touch-up milestones
  • Bid due date, walkthrough date, and substantial completion
  • Site safety, orientation, insurance, and closeout requirements

For recurring contracts, include

  • Building type, operating hours, and occupancy
  • Room list, floor areas, restroom count, and traffic levels
  • Required frequencies, service days, and time windows
  • Consumables, equipment, waste, and supply responsibilities
  • Inspection cadence, escalation contacts, and reporting
A submitted inquiry is not an accepted bid or contract. Work is scheduled only after written scope approval, pricing acceptance, contract/purchase order, and mobilization confirmation.
