Commercial delivery model

Project-based engagements

Used for construction cleaning, one-time deep cleans, turnover events, and defined property projects. Scope is tied to the work areas, project phase, schedule, and completion criteria.

  • Bid package and scope review
  • Site walkthrough when required
  • Labor and schedule planning
  • Task completion and punch-list coordination

Recurring service agreements

Used for janitorial, multifamily common areas, parking lot pickup, and valet trash. The agreement should identify frequency, service days, task lists, exclusions, reporting, and escalation contacts.

  • Site-specific service specification
  • Routine schedule and access plan
  • Inspection and issue-resolution cadence
  • Periodic scope review
