Resources for commercial clients

Practical information for planning a cleaning scope or preparing a bid.

Use these checklists to assemble the information that helps contractors, property managers, and facility teams obtain a complete and comparable proposal.

Construction bid package checklist

Plans, specifications, area schedules, finish schedule, project schedule, bid forms, alternates, and cleaning acceptance criteria.

Commercial janitorial scope checklist

Building hours, occupancy, room list, floor types, restroom count, frequencies, consumables, security, and inspection expectations.

Multifamily service checklist

Unit count, common-area inventory, amenity spaces, service days, trash procedures, resident rules, and manager escalation contact.

Vendor onboarding checklist

Legal entity details, W-9, insurance requirements, safety documentation, vendor forms, payment setup, and purchase-order process.

Site walkthrough checklist

Access, parking, water/power, storage, work windows, loading areas, restricted zones, waste disposal, and site contact.

Closeout checklist

Completed areas, inspection notes, outstanding items, authorized re-cleans, sign-off, invoice backup, and warranty/return visit terms.

These resources are planning aids. The executed contract, project specifications, and applicable law govern the actual work and responsibilities.
