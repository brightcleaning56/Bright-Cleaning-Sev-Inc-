Frequently asked questions

Questions about scopes, bids, and service.

Common information for commercial customers and construction project teams.

What information should I include in a commercial cleaning bid request?

Include the project address, service scope, square footage, plans/specifications, bid due date, target start date, cleaning phases, access requirements, and site contact.

Can Bright Cleaning Sev Inc review construction plans?

The bid request form accepts common document formats for review. File types and size limits are enforced by the website.

Do you provide one-time and recurring services?

Both project-based and recurring service options may be considered. Availability and scope are confirmed for each opportunity.

Do you offer site walkthroughs?

Walkthroughs can be requested. Timing and availability are confirmed with the project contact.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, square footage, building use, surface conditions, schedule, frequency, access, supplies/equipment, and project requirements.

Does submitting a request confirm a booking?

No. Submission is an inquiry only. Work begins only after scope, pricing, schedule, and contract terms are approved in writing.
