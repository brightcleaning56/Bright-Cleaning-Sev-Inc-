Vendor & procurement information

Ready to coordinate vendor onboarding and bid requirements.

A central point for procurement teams, general contractors, property managers, and owners to identify the information needed before award and mobilization.

Vendor qualification

Documents should be current, accurate, and project-specific.

The exact package depends on the customer’s vendor onboarding process, contract terms, project location, and insurance requirements.

Company & tax information

Legal entity name, business address, W-9, remittance information, authorized contacts, and vendor registration forms.

Insurance documentation

Current certificate of insurance and required endorsements/limits, as specified by the contract or customer.

Safety & site compliance

Site orientation, hazard communication, task-specific training, PPE expectations, incident reporting, and project safety documentation as required.

Procurement & contract setup

Executed agreement, purchase order, scope exhibits, schedule, billing portal, invoice backup, change-order authorization, and payment terms.

Project references & capability

Approved project descriptions, references, service locations, capacity, and relevant experience where requested and authorized.

Mobilization information

Site contacts, access badges, work hours, parking, staging, storage, utilities, waste handling, and emergency procedures.

Pre-award and mobilization checklist

Legal business name and W-9 match contract entity
Certificate of insurance reviewed against project limits
Required endorsements and additional insured wording confirmed
Vendor registration and tax forms completed
Safety orientation and site rules received
Scope, exclusions, alternates, and unit prices approved
Purchase order or written authorization issued
Schedule, site access, and mobilization date confirmed
Invoice submission process and payment terms confirmed
Named project contacts and escalation path established
Do not assume a certificate of insurance alone satisfies contract requirements. Review the actual contract and required endorsements with the broker and customer.
